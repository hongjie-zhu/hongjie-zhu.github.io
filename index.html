About me

I am an undergraduate student in Biomedical Engineering in the joint programme between Northeastern University and the University of Dundee. I am currently completing my final undergraduate year at Dundee and expect to graduate in June 2027.

My research centres on medical artificial intelligence, with current work spanning dynamic ultrasound interpretation, real-time localisation of lesions and interventional instruments, multimodal clinical decision support, and cross-scale analysis of brain imaging and histology. These projects include model development, independent or prospective testing, model interpretation, and reader studies, with an emphasis on evaluation that reflects clinical use.

I am interested in reliable and interpretable intelligent systems that can fit into clinical workflows. I have also worked on visual perception and control for medical robotics. Looking ahead, I hope to explore connections among medical imaging, tissue structure, and molecular and cellular states, and to translate engineering methods into tools with practical medical value.
