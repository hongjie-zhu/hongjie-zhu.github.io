01 / DYNAMIC ULTRASOUNDPublished · 2026

Following motion.
Supporting clinical decisions.

A workflow-oriented system for Green classification of cystocele. Green-AttGRU combines urethrovesical junction localization with temporal modeling across the Valsalva sequence.

  • Temporal modeling
  • Reader study
  • Prospective validation
Study details +

The study included 881 patients: 688 in retrospective development and 193 in independent prospective testing. A nested 67-patient reader study assessed AI assistance for four radiologists.

In the reader study, inter-reader agreement increased from κ = 0.453 to 0.786, and pooled median interpretation time decreased from 26.7 to 9.9 seconds. These findings concern imaging interpretation; they do not establish downstream patient outcomes.

02 / REAL-TIME PERCEPTIONResearch project

Tracking lesions and instruments
as anatomy changes.

A lightweight framework for simultaneous lesion and instrument-tip localization during ultrasound-guided vacuum-assisted breast tumor resection, designed for changing lesion size, deformation, and motion.

  • Object detection
  • Efficient inference
  • Ultrasound guidance
Study details +

Detector-VABTR combines MobileNetV4 with dynamic receptive-field attention and multi-scale feature fusion. The study used 167 patients and 10,668 annotated keyframes, with patient-level data separation.

The supplied manuscript reports lesion / instrument-tip mAP50 of 0.861 / 0.796 on the held-out test set. Clinical usability was evaluated through an offline playback reader study.

03 / MULTIMODAL REASONINGResearch project

Bringing imaging and
clinical context together.

LRM-Fusion integrates contrast-enhanced ultrasound sequences with clinical variables to distinguish hepatocellular carcinoma from non-HCC malignancies within malignant CEUS LI-RADS LR-M lesions.

  • Multimodal fusion
  • Calibration
  • Interpretability
Study details +

The supplied revised manuscript describes a 265-patient retrospective cohort and a 99-patient multicenter test cohort, combining 61 prospectively accrued patients with 38 external patients.

The fusion model achieved an AUC of 0.898 in the multicenter test cohort. Evaluation included calibration, decision curve analysis, interpretability, and a four-radiologist reader study.

04 / VERIFIABLE GENERATIONResearch project

Connecting MRI to
tissue-scale evidence.

TriSCoV-Net explores diffusion MRI-to-immunomarker proxy mapping under weak registration, using structural priors and cross-scale consistency to support inspectable, quantifiable outputs.

  • Virtual histology
  • Conditional generation
  • Cross-scale learning
Study details +

The framework combines dMRI-conditioned generation, PLI-assisted conditional super-resolution, a support-domain constraint, and cross-scale fine-tuning. Evaluation uses subject-level leave-one-subject-out testing on the Oxford Digital Brain Bank.

The primary output is a tissue-scale immunomarker proxy at 16.8 μm/pixel. These maps are intended for MRI–histology association and fixed-protocol quantification, rather than recovery of individual cells or replacement of physical IHC staining.
